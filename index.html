Welcome to Kathpencil

A colorful space where creativity meets expression. We offer professional courses in sketching, watercolors, oil painting, acrylics, and more—designed for budding artists of all ages. Our mission is to nurture every student’s imagination and technique in a fun, vibrant environment.

🎨 What You Can Learn at Kathpencil

Sketching ✏️

Watercolour 💧

Pastel & Acrylic 🎨

Master brushwork and bold colours in both traditional and modern painting styles.

About Kathpencil

At KATHPENCIL, art meets imagination! Founded with a vision to inspire budding artists, our institute offers a vibrant and nurturing space for learners of all ages to explore the magical world of drawing, painting, and visual storytelling.

Whether you're picking up a pencil for the first time or refining your skills, Kathpencil helps you grow with confidence and creativity.

From fundamental sketching to masterclasses in watercolor, oil, and acrylic techniques — we blend tradition with experimentation. Our mentors believe in not just teaching art, but nurturing the artist in every heart.
